`riesz_log` with the zero, nearest or mirror boundary does the LoG and the Riesz transform in one FFT round trip on one padded image. Before, it did two round trips with a float32 cut between them. On a 194x198x193 IBSI 2 phase 3 CT scan, it takes 0.40 of the time at 10 threads and at 1 thread. It needs 0.39 of the peak memory. The values change toward the boundary condition. On that scan the change is 7.4e-5 of the response range with the zero boundary, 6.9e-3 with nearest and 1.7e-2 with mirror. In a comparison with a reference of each boundary, the new values were as close or closer in all 66 measured cases. The periodic boundary (the default) does not change. The IBSI 2 tests 9.a and 9.b.1 pass with the same errors. A `sigma_mm`, `spacing_mm` or `truncate` that is not a finite positive number now raises a ValueError before the padding.
