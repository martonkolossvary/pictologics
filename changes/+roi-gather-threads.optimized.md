The ROI values of a large mask box are now gathered in parallel, with 4 threads or more. `apply_mask` and the pipeline read a box of 2^20 voxels or more in place, with no bool mask and no copy of the box. The values and their order do not change. On a 512x512x200 CT at 10 threads, `apply_mask` with a lesion mask takes 3.4 ms instead of 11.8 ms and 2.4 MB instead of 50 MB. The standard_fbn_32 configuration of an IBSI 2 MRI scan (a box of 10.7 million voxels) takes 84 ms instead of 93 ms.
