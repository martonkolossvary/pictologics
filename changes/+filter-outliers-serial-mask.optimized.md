`filter_outliers` makes the new mask in one serial pass with no branch when the image has fewer than 2^20 voxels, and it copies the ROI values only when they hold a NaN. On the IBSI 1 configuration D crop (63x61x56 voxels) the step takes 0.14 ms instead of 0.28 ms. On a 1 mm CT lesion crop it takes 0.09 ms instead of 0.18 ms. With one thread, a 512x512x200 CT takes 37 ms instead of 45 ms. The results are the same, bit for bit. When the image and the mask are both in column order, the new mask is in column order too.
