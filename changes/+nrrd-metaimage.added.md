`load_image` now reads NRRD files (`.nrrd`, `.nhdr` with a detached data file, and 3D Slicer `.seg.nrrd`) and MetaImage files (`.mha`, `.mhd`), with its own readers. The geometry is in the LPS+ frame, from the RAS, LAS or LPS space of a NRRD file. A 4D file or the layers of a `.seg.nrrd` file give the volume of `dataset_index`. `get_segment_info` lists the segments of a `.seg.nrrd` file with their label values and layers. On 60 files that SimpleITK wrote and on `.seg.nrrd` files of 3D Slicer, the arrays and the geometry are the same as SimpleITK and Slicer give.
