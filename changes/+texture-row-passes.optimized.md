The GLCM pairs and the GLRLM runs of the local texture kernel count in passes over the contiguous ROI segments of each row (a segment shorter than 8 voxels goes voxel by voxel, as before), and up to 64 grey levels the GLCM pairs alternate between two tables. The counts are the same, bit for bit. All six texture families take x0.85-0.89 of the time at 10 threads on ROIs of 0.6-0.7 million voxels (GLCM + GLRLM x0.69-0.75) and x0.77-0.79 at 1 thread (GLCM + GLRLM x0.61-0.62).
