The mean, Gaussian and Laws filters and the LoG passes run their 1-D passes in numba kernels. The kernels keep the arithmetic of scipy and read the planes and rows in memory order, so the values are the same bits. At 10 threads a 256^3 image takes 2-3 times less time: mean 28 -> 10 ms, Laws rotation invariant 177 -> 68 ms (average pooling 253 -> 96 ms). At the CT pipeline shapes the filters are 1.3-1.4 times faster, also at 1 thread (Gaussian 64 -> 50 ms). The pooling steps of the Laws rotation invariance and the adds of the LoG passes run in threads from 6 MB on.
