From 131,072 zones on, the GLSZM and GLDZM tables of the pipeline and of `calculate_all_texture_features` are counted in threads, one chunk of zones in each thread. The zone pass also adds to the zone roots only the zones that a chunk face joins, not all zones. At 256 grey levels on 10 threads, the GLSZM and GLDZM of a 150^3 ROI cube take 10.2 ms instead of 16.4 ms, and all texture families take 16.8 ms instead of 22.8 ms. Below 131,072 zones and at 1 thread the time stays the same. The values are the same, bit for bit.
