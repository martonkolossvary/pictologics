The filters with a source mask (the `roi_only` and `auto` source modes) now use parallel numba kernels. The kernels make the masked image, the weight and the normalized response. Each kernel reads the arrays once. The wavelet filter casts the masked image to float32 in the same copy. On a 232x182x148 image with 30 % invalid voxels at 10 threads, the mean filter takes 13.0 ms instead of 39.1 ms. The LoG takes 30.1 ms instead of 55.8 ms, and the wavelet 4.8 ms instead of 22.5 ms. On one thread, the mean filter takes 63.1 ms instead of 71.4 ms. On a 512x512x200 CT, a pipeline with seven masked filters takes 271 ms instead of 350 ms. A masked wavelet needs 10 % less memory. The values are the same, bit for bit.
