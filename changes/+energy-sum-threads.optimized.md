The energy and the root mean square of the first-order family now sum their squares in threads from about one million values. Each block of 4,096 values sums in the same order as before, and the block sums add in block order. So the values are the same as in 0.8.0, bit for bit, at every number of threads. The sum of 52 million values takes 1.9 ms instead of 9.6 ms at 10 threads. One thread is 1.5 times faster. The first-order family of a whole 512×512×200 CT grid takes 36 ms instead of 44 ms.
