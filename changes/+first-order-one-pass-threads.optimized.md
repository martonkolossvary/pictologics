The first-order features of a ROI with 524,288 values or more take the moments and the three absolute deviations from one pass in threads, in 64 fixed parts. The first-order features of 2.3 million values (an IBSI 2 CT scan at 1 mm) take 2.5 ms instead of 3.9 ms on 10 threads; one thread is as fast as before. The values do not depend on the number of threads. The variance, skewness, kurtosis, coefficient of variation and the three absolute deviations of these ROIs move by less than 1e-10 (relative; at most 1.3e-11 in the IBSI 2 phase 3 data) and are closer to the exact sums. Smaller ROIs give the same values as before, bit for bit.
