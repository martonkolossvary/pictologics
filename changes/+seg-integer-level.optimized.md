`load_seg` compares the frames of a DICOM SEG with an integer level. The stored values are integers, so a level of 127.5 is the same as 128. Before, the float level changed each frame to float64 for the compare. The masks are the same, bit for bit. A BINARY SEG of 258 frames (512 x 512, 20 segments) loads in 0.56 of the time as a label map and in 0.80 as a dict of masks, at 10 threads. A FRACTIONAL SEG of 300 frames loads in 0.62 of the time.
