The mean filter and the Laws energy sum each window on its own instead of a running sum. The result is the exact mean of the float32 values, rounded once to double and then to float32. So a value does not depend on where the image starts. Values can move by one float32 unit in rare voxels. The IBSI reference values do not change. In the IBSI 2 phase 3 data, 49 of 24,786 values of the mean filter move by at most 3e-12. The pipeline now filters only the ROI box plus the support for these filters. The mean filter step of a CT run takes 0.5 ms instead of 4.3 ms. A CT run with a rotation-invariant Laws energy takes 30 ms instead of 55 ms. A standalone mean filter of support 15 is 1.2 to 1.6 times slower on an image below 128^3 voxels or at 1 thread.
