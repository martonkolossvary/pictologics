The mean filter and the Laws energy sum each window on its own (the exact mean of the float32 values, rounded once to double and then to float32) instead of a running sum, so a value does not depend on where the image starts. Values can move by one float32 unit in rare voxels (IBSI reference values unchanged; in the IBSI 2 phase 3 data, 49 of 24,786 values of the mean filter move by at most 2e-12). The pipeline now filters only the ROI box plus the support for these filters: the mean filter step of a CT run takes 0.5 ms instead of 4.3 ms, and a CT run with a rotation-invariant Laws energy takes 30 ms instead of 55 ms. A standalone mean filter of support 15 on an image below 128^3 voxels or at 1 thread is 1.2-1.35 times slower.
