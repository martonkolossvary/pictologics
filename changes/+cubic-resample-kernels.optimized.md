Cubic (tricubic spline) resampling of float64 images now runs on parallel numba kernels. The kernels give the output of scipy bit for bit, also in the region form of the pipeline. The IBSI 2 CT phantom to 1 mm takes 20 ms instead of 957 ms (161 ms on one thread). A 512x512x200 CT to 1 mm takes 146 ms instead of 5.5 s, with 40 % less peak memory. This is also true with a source mask (sentinel voxels): the IBSI 2 CT phantom with a sentinel mask takes 40 ms instead of 1.9 s, and the CT takes 257 ms instead of 10.8 s with 45 % less peak memory.
