The rotation-invariant wavelets with a mixed decomposition (such as LHL) take the tree of shared passes from 2^21 voxels on (before: from 2^23 and with average pooling only), also with max and min pooling, whose pooling steps run in threads in fixed chunks of planes. At 10 threads this takes x0.66-0.70 of the time (IBSI 2 phase 3 configuration 6 on a CT scan 53 -> 36 ms, a 256^3 image with max pooling 143 -> 95 ms), at 1 thread x0.56, with 27 % less memory. The tree adds the passes in another order, so the values of a mixed decomposition of 2^21 to 2^23 voxels (any pooling) and of max or min pooling above change in the last float32 bits (IBSI 2 phase 3 configuration 6 on 54 of 153 scans; no verdict changes).
