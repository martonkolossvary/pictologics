The rotation-invariant wavelets with a mixed decomposition (such as LHL) take the tree of shared passes from 2^21 voxels on. Before, the tree started at 2^23 voxels, with average pooling only. Max and min pooling now take it too, and their pooling steps run in threads in fixed chunks of planes. At 10 threads this takes x0.66-0.70 of the time. IBSI 2 phase 3 configuration 6 on a CT scan takes 36 ms instead of 53 ms. A 256^3 image with max pooling takes 95 ms instead of 143 ms. At 1 thread it takes x0.56 of the time, with 27 % less memory. The tree adds the passes in another order. So the last float32 bits change for a mixed decomposition of 2^21 to 2^23 voxels (any pooling), and above that for max or min pooling. In IBSI 2 phase 3, configuration 6 changes on 54 of 153 scans, with no verdict changes.
