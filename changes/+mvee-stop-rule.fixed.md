The minimum volume enclosing ellipsoid (MVEE) of the morphology features now stops on the Mahalanobis gap, with away steps (Wolfe-Atwood), at eps = 1e-3 (the volume is then within about 0.1 % of the minimum). The old stop rule left the ellipsoid 0.3 to 0.5 % too small, so `volume_density_mvee_SWZ1` and `area_density_mvee_BRI8` were 0.2 to 0.5 % too large; they move by that much now (IBSI gives no reference for them). The MVEE of a CT lesion hull of 680 points takes about 4 ms instead of 0.4 ms; in `run()` it stays hidden behind the texture pass.
