The GLCM, GLRLM, GLSZM, GLDZM and NGLDM features come from one compiled kernel for each family. The kernels add the terms in the order of numpy (pairwise sums), so the features are the same, bit for bit. The pass that makes the grey-level volume also gives the texture voxel count and the grey-level span of the GLCM. So the feature functions make no more passes over the ROI. On one thread, a small ROI counts the GLCM in one table, and its tables need no sum over the threads. `calculate_all_texture_features` of an ROI of 1 to 3,000 voxels takes 0.27 to 0.58 of the time. `run_rois()` of 200 small ROIs with the texture features takes 35 ms instead of 71 ms, and with all families 76 ms instead of 115 ms. All six families of a large ROI take about 0.91 of the time, and the GLCM alone about 0.88. A two-dimensional GLCM or GLRLM table given to its feature function now counts as one table. Before, it gave an error.
