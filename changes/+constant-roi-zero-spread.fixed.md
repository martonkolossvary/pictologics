The first-order features of a ROI whose values are all equal now give exactly 0 for the variance, the three absolute deviations and the coefficient of variation, as IBSI defines them. Before, a value that is not exact in binary (0.1 or 1/3, for example) gave rounding noise: a variance of about 1e-34 and a robust mean absolute deviation of up to 3e-13. Equal infinite values do not change.
