Moran's I and Geary's C choose between the pair loop and the FFT with a fixed count of 4 threads in their time model. Before, the choice used the thread count of the import. So the choice, and thus the values, are now the same on every computer. At 1 thread, for example in the workers of run_batch, a compact ROI of 3,400 voxels takes 0.84 ms instead of 5.9 ms. At 10 threads, some ROIs of 2,000 to 5,000 voxels take up to 0.8 ms more. The values of these ROIs move by at most 4e-15 (relative). The IBSI values stay the same, bit for bit.
