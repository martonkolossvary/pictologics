Rotation-invariant wavelet filters use their threads from 15,000 voxels and Laws filters from 20,000 voxels, not from 2 million voxels. Below 2 million voxels, the 24 rotations ran one after the other, although the threads give the same result, bit for bit. With the IBSI 2 phase 3 settings, a Coiflet 3 wavelet on images of 262,000 to 1.1 million voxels takes 7-9x less time (1.58 s to 0.17 s), also in the pipeline, and the Laws energy filter takes 1.7-2.2x less time. Each thread holds one working array, so these images need more memory than one rotation at a time (for example 35 MB to 133 MB).
