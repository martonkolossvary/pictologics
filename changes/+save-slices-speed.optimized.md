`save_slices` saves up to 8 slices at a time in threads, as RGB instead of RGBA (the alpha channel was always 255, because the mask overlay is mixed into the colors), and with PNG compression level 3 instead of 6. The pixels are the same, and JPEG files are the same byte for byte. 40 slices of a 512×512×200 CT at the default 300 dpi take 0.64 s instead of 17.6 s, with 3% smaller files and about 120 MB more peak memory; at 72 dpi they take 0.12 s instead of 1.4 s, with 30% larger PNG files. TIFF files are 25% smaller.
