The IBSI 2 phase 3 page now uses one range per team, modality, filter and feature. The old page used one range per team and feature for all modalities and filters, and this made the agreement too high. Within 1 % of the range, CERR agrees in 89.4 % of the values instead of 99.3 %, and NCT Dresden in 98.2 % instead of 99.5 %. The IBSI 2 manual lists the mirror boundary for the Simoncelli filters, and the page explains why Pictologics uses the periodic boundary. With the periodic boundary, 58.1 % of the Simoncelli values are within 1 % of the range; with the mirror boundary, 56.3 % are. The page lists the 10 largest mismatches per team instead of all mismatches (14 KB instead of 524 KB).
