The FFT LoG of a float64 image of 2 million padded voxels or more no longer builds the padded image or the float64 response: each thread pads its slab of planes a chunk at a time and transforms it into the spectrum, the transfer product runs in threads, and the inverse transforms only the planes of the result. A 256³ image takes 34 ms instead of 47 ms at 10 threads (193 instead of 236 ms at 1 thread) with 262 instead of 366 MB of peak memory over the input, and a 512×512×200 CT 82 ms instead of 119 ms with 707 instead of 968 MB. The values are the same, bit for bit (with SciPy 1.18 or later the last digits can differ, as they already do with the number of threads).
