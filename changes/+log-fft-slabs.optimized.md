The FFT LoG of a float64 image of 2 million padded voxels or more no longer builds the padded image or the float64 response. Each thread pads its slab of planes a chunk at a time and transforms it into the spectrum. The transfer product runs in threads. The inverse transform makes only the planes of the result. A 256^3 image takes 34 ms instead of 47 ms at 10 threads (193 ms instead of 236 ms at 1 thread). Its peak memory over the input is 262 MB instead of 366 MB. A 512x512x200 CT takes 82 ms instead of 119 ms, with 707 MB instead of 968 MB. The values are the same, bit for bit. With SciPy 1.18 or later, the last digits can differ, as they already do with the number of threads.
