The convex hull of the morphology features finds its candidate vertices on the voxel grid, not by a pass over all mesh vertices. The candidates are the voxels that end their rows, kept by their 2-D plane hulls, and then the mesh vertices next to them. The hull step of a 150^3 ROI takes 0.45 ms instead of 3.4 ms, with 2.3 MB instead of 6.9 MB of peak memory. The hull step of an STS CT lesion takes 1.6 ms instead of 2.9 ms, with 1.7 MB instead of 3.4 MB. The hull vertices, the maximum 3-D diameter and the MVEE features are the same, bit for bit. The two hull densities move by at most 2e-15 (relative) for a region near the grid origin.
