The convex hull of the morphology features finds its candidate vertices on the voxel grid (the voxels that end their rows, their 2-D plane hulls, and the mesh vertices next to them), not by a pass over all mesh vertices. A 150^3 ROI takes 4.0 ms instead of 7.0 ms, a 160x430x390 body 17.5 ms instead of 23.5 ms, and an STS CT lesion 4.8 ms instead of 6.4 ms; small ROIs are unchanged. The hull vertices, the maximum 3-D diameter and the MVEE features are the same, bit for bit; the two hull densities move by at most 1e-15 (relative) for a region near the grid origin.
