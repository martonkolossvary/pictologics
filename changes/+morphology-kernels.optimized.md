The morphology features take 10-65 % less time on ROIs of every size, and 15-22 % less peak memory: the moments kernel has no branch, the mesh vertex statistics use scalar accumulators, the maximum diameter of a hull below 640 vertices runs in one thread, the parallel marching cubes walks its planes in one block per thread, the MVEE reads four points per step, and the ROI box is copied once with its border. A 150^3 ROI takes 6.9 ms instead of 8.4 ms, a 1-voxel ROI 0.05 ms instead of 0.15 ms, and the 20 small ROIs of a CT label map 8.5 ms instead of 12.5 ms. The values are the same, bit for bit.
