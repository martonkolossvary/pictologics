`save_slices` now builds each slice as RGB in one pass, mixes the mask colors only into the labelled pixels and scales the gray values in place. For a volume of 8.4 M voxels or more, it finds the gray range in threads. The files are the same byte for byte. On 10 threads, 10 slices of a 512×512×200 CT with a 20-label mask take 17 ms instead of 29 ms (97 ms instead of 119 ms on one thread), with 35 % less peak memory. All 200 slices take 0.22 s instead of 0.30 s.
