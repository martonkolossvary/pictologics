Without `window_center` and `window_width`, `save_slices` and `visualize_slices` show all slices on one gray scale: the minimum and maximum of the whole volume, NaN values left out. Before, each slice was scaled by its own minimum and maximum, so a tissue changed its gray level from slice to slice and while scrolling. A mask shown in gray uses the range of the whole mask. NaN pixels show as black instead of going through an undefined NaN-to-integer cast.
