`keep_largest_component` no longer labels a mask that fills its bounding box, because such a mask is one component. When a run has no mask, the pipeline makes a full mask, and this step was slow. On a 512x512x200 CT, the step now takes 7 ms, not 440 ms, and 100 MB of peak memory, not 300 MB. The step also labels a 1-byte copy of the mask, so it takes about 10 % less time on a float64 mask. The results are the same, bit for bit.
