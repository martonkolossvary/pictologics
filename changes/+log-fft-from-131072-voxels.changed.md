The LoG of a float64 image of 131,072 voxels (about 51^3) or more is now one float64 FFT convolution. Before, images below 64^3 voxels ran eight float32 passes. The values of these images move by up to 3e-7 of the largest response, toward the float64 values. At 10 threads such an image takes x0.31 to x0.51 of the time (58x57x58: 1.8 ms instead of 4.0 ms). At 1 thread it takes x0.68 to x0.97.
