Label-map DICOM SEG files (Label Map Segmentation Storage, SOP class 1.2.840.10008.5.1.4.1.1.66.7) now load as segmentations. `load_image` sent them to the plain DICOM loader, and `load_seg` read their frames as binary masks, so every label became 1. Now their pixel values are the segment numbers: the combined mask keeps them, and each separate mask is the voxels of one number. The background, segment 0, is left out unless it is asked for.
