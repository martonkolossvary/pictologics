The GLDZM no longer drops zones silently: a texture voxel outside the distance mask got distance 0, and its zone left the matrix. A direct call with such masks now raises a `ValueError` with the number of voxels outside. In the pipeline, `keep_largest_component` and `binarize_mask` with `apply_to="morph"` now also keep the intensity mask inside the new morphological mask (in IBSI, the intensity mask lies inside the morphological mask), so the features of a multi-component ROI come from the kept component alone.
