The GLDZM no longer drops zones silently. Before, a texture voxel outside the distance mask got distance 0, and its zone left the matrix. A direct call with such masks now raises a `ValueError` with the number of voxels outside. In the pipeline, `keep_largest_component` and `binarize_mask` with `apply_to="morph"` now also keep the intensity mask inside the new morphological mask. In IBSI, the intensity mask lies inside the morphological mask. So the features of a multi-component ROI come from the kept component alone. When the two masks were one array, the intensity mask after the step is the new morphological mask, as with `apply_to="both"`. Else the intersection runs on the box of the new mask: about 2 ms on a 512x512x200 CT, not about 20 ms.
