The documentation of the FFT filters (Simoncelli, Riesz) now gives the measured accuracy of their pad with a non-periodic boundary. Against a 64-voxel pad, the default pads differ by up to about 1e-1 of the response range. This occurs at the border of a CT scan and at the centre of a large uniform object. The earlier claim (99.9 % of the kernel's L2 energy inside the pad) holds, but the L2 energy is the wrong measure of this error. The periodic boundary, the IBSI default, takes no pad.
