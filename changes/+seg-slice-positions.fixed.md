DICOM SEG files now load each frame into the slice at its position. Before, the loader took the first dimension index value as the slice number, but highdicom puts the segment number there, so each segment collapsed into one slice (in a test, a segment on 4 slices came back on 1 slice with a quarter of its voxels). The SEG origin and slice spacing now also come from the frame positions, also when the writer leaves out empty frames.
