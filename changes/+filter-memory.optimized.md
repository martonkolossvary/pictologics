Filters use less memory. The parallel path of rotation-invariant wavelets kept every finished rotation until the end (all 24 responses at once); it now drops each response once it is pooled and runs at most one rotation per core. The Simoncelli and Riesz transfer-function caches kept up to 32 and 64 full volumes, which grew without a byte limit over a cohort of images with different shapes; they now keep at most 2 GB each. On images of 2 to 2.5 million voxels, rotation-invariant wavelets take 10-14% less time and 29-31% less memory, and the IBSI 2 phase 3 run uses 22% less memory.
