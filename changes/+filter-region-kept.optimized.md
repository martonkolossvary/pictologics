A filter that computes only the ROI region now keeps that region. The pipeline cuts the image and the masks to it, as after a discretise step. Before, it wrote the region into a zeros array of the whole grid. The case is a 512x512x200 CT with a 20 mm lesion. A 1 mm resample with a LoG filter takes 18 ms instead of 27 ms at 10 threads. At 1 thread it takes 103 ms instead of 112 ms. Its peak memory is 279 MB instead of 400 MB. A LoG without a resample takes 7 ms instead of 21 ms. With a grow_mask step and the local intensity peaks, it takes 25 ms instead of 56 ms. The features are the same, bit for bit.
