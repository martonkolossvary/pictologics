A filter that computes only the ROI region now keeps that region: the pipeline cuts the image and the masks to it, as after a discretise step, instead of writing the region into a zeros array of the whole grid. On a 512x512x200 CT with a 20 mm lesion, a 1 mm resample with a LoG filter takes 18 ms instead of 27 ms at 10 threads (103 instead of 112 ms at 1 thread) and 279 MB instead of 400 MB; a LoG without a resample takes 7 ms instead of 21 ms; with a grow_mask step and the local intensity peaks, 25 ms instead of 56 ms. The features are the same, bit for bit.
