`load_image(stored_type=True)` keeps the integer type of a NIfTI mask or label map with no scaling, and the pipeline loads its masks and label maps with it. A 512x512x200 uint8 mask loads in less time with 100 MB instead of 450 MB of peak memory, and `run_rois` with a uint8 label map file takes a third of the time with 100 MB instead of 850 MB.
