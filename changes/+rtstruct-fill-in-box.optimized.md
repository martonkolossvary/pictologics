`load_rtstruct()` fills each ROI only in the box of its contour spans. It makes no full-size mask for an ROI and does not scan one for its box. On a 512x512x200 CT with 20 small ROIs, the label image takes 24 ms instead of 73 ms and 51 MB instead of 101 MB of memory. The dict of binary masks takes 60 ms instead of 72 ms. At its return, the process holds 10 MB instead of 1 GB more resident memory, because the zero pages of the masks stay unmapped. The masks are the same, bit for bit.
