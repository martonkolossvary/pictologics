Deduplication no longer copies wrong values between configurations. Before, the reuse check ignored some settings, so a configuration could get the values of another configuration. The check ignored `source_mode` and `sentinel_value`. It ignored the `keep_largest_component`, `binarize_mask` and `round_intensities` steps, and a `filter` step before morphology features. It ignored `extract_features` options, for example `ivh_params`, `texture_matrix_params` and `include_spatial_intensity`. It also ignored a step after `discretise` and a second `extract_features` step. Now a family is reused only when the source mode, every preprocessing step before extraction and the extraction options are the same. Only a final `discretise` step can differ, for families that do not use the binned image. An `ivh_use_continuous` flag inside `ivh_params` no longer removes the discretisation dependency of IVH, because feature extraction ignores that flag. The fix applies to every rules version, also to a pinned `"1.0.0"`. Results with `deduplicate=True` now equal results with `deduplicate=False`. Configurations that differ only in a final discretisation step still share their morphology and intensity features.
